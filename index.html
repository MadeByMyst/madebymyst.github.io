Why simple?

Most people decide in a few seconds whether to stay on a website. So I keep things simple on purpose: one clear message, pages that load fast on any phone, and plain code that's easy to change later.

  • Clear

    Visitors understand what you do and what to click next, without hunting for it.

  • Fast

    No frameworks or heavy add-ons, so pages stay light even on a slow connection.

  • Easy to change

    Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that any developer can read and update.

How it works

Three steps, and you'll always know where things stand.

  1. We talk

    You tell me about your project and show me a few sites you like. I'll ask questions until I understand what you need.

  2. I build it

    I design and build the site around your brand, and check it on phones, tablets and desktops as I go.

  3. You review, we launch

    You ask for changes until it's right. Then I hand over clean, commented code, ready to go live.

To get started, I'll need your text and images, your logo if you have one, colours you like and a few websites you like.

Common questions

Can't find yours? Ask me directly.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your text and images, your logo if you have one, any colour preferences, and a few websites you like. That's enough to begin.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what they should do. Send me a short description and I'll reply with a price for your project.

How long does it take?

That depends on the size of the site. You'll get an estimated timeline together with the price, before any work starts.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site I build is responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Do you provide hosting or a domain?

No, you'll need your own. If you're not sure where to start, I'll guide you through the setup.

Can you add more pages later?

Yes. Extra pages are available as an add-on whenever you're ready to grow.

Do you build online stores?

Not at the moment. I focus on landing pages and business websites. If you need e-commerce, message me first and we'll talk about what's possible.

Do you work with clients outside Oman?

Yes. I work remotely through Fiverr, Upwork and Freelancer, or directly by email.

Can we talk before I place an order?

Please do. A quick chat first helps us agree on what you need and makes everything smoother for both of us.
